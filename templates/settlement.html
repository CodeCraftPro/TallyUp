How it's calculated

Simple expenses: Amount is divided equally among selected consumers. Payer gets credited.

Restaurant bills: Offer discount is shared by all diners. Taxes (GST) are split fairly. Advance cover charge is credited to whoever booked. Dineout coins are treated as personal wallet savings (reimbursed by the group) or shared group credits.

Itemized bills: Each person's share is proportional to their individual items. Any difference between items total and bill is distributed proportionally.

Finally, debts are simplified into the fewest transactions possible.
